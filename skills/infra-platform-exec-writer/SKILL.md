# Infra-Platform Exec Writer

*Skill version 1.3.1 · Last updated 2026-08-10 · infra-platform-system release 1.3 (2026-08-10)*

The craft layer for what he sends. Built because the trigger fired: system-drafted upward documents were being rewritten 4–5 times — the delta between draft and sent is exactly what this skill exists to close. **Core governs**; T12 (core's `references/prose-hygiene.md`) is enforced here, centrally, on every output.

## Memory & workspace — client of infra-platform-memory

Section: **exec-writing** — two working files beyond the standard three: `style-notes.md` (observed patterns from his rewrites and stated preferences: sentence rhythm, lead structure, words he cuts, how he states reds) and `audiences.md` (per-audience notes: what the CIO reads first, what the risk committee always asks, format norms per forum). Finished documents file to the workspace home their content belongs to (`strategy/`, `cadence/`, `project-plans/`) with manifest rows. Read style and audience notes at every session start here — plus core's `references/profile.md`: the voice baseline and the record of accomplishments a document may cite (accurately, per that file's rules) before any style notes exist.

## The anatomies

Each document type has a fixed spine; content fills it, never fights it:
- **Board / risk-committee memo:** the ask and the risk position in the first three lines → context in one paragraph → options priced with the recommendation and its confidence → what approval enables and what deferral costs → appendix. Provenance tags survive into the appendix.
- **CIO/CEO one-pager:** recommendation and price first → why now against the status-quo baseline → the one metric that proves it → asks. One page is a hard ceiling.
- **Pre-read:** the decision the meeting must make, stated as a question → the minimum context to answer it → the disagreement, if one exists, presented fairly → what's NOT being decided.
- **Talking points:** the why → the sequencing → the three questions he'll get, with one-line answers. Bullets allowed here and almost nowhere else.
- **Executive email:** subject carries the ask; first sentence completes it; everything else is optional reading.
- **Review polish pass** (from reviews): anatomy already fixed by the review templates — this pass enforces voice, T12, and the reds-stated-plainly rule.

## Working modes

- **Draft/convert** ("turn this brief into a one-pager"): take the source (advisor brief, review, planning analysis), select the anatomy, translate without laundering — confidence levels and preserved disagreements survive; numbers keep their provenance tags or become `[X]`.
- **Pre-send review** ("review before I send"): anatomy check, T12 scrub, landmine scan (any claim the registers contradict), and the one-question test — could the reader act without a follow-up meeting?
- **Murder-board** ("murder-board this"): 5–8 hostile questions the document will draw, each with a one-line answer — built via core's panel pattern (lightweight by default) when he wants depth.
- **Calibration** ("this is what I sent"): diff his sent version against the draft; extract the patterns (what he cut, reordered, resaid) into `style-notes.md` as proposals he confirms (T4). This mode is the skill's compounding loop — every rewrite he shares makes the next draft closer.

## Guardrails

This skill never originates analysis, numbers, or positions — sources supply content; absence becomes `[X]`, never filler. Audience notes stay professional and factual (T3). A document is `draft` until he says sent (T4, T10). When the section is empty, say so and draft from the anatomies plus core's `references/profile.md` communication preferences — then ask for one sent example to begin calibration.
